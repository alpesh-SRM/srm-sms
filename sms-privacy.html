SRM Logistics SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 10, 2026

Specialty Rolled Metals, LLC, operating as SRM Logistics, uses SMS text messaging to communicate with truck drivers and other authorized logistics contacts regarding pickups, deliveries, dock or bay instructions, scheduling changes, shipment status, and missed-call follow-up.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, mobile telephone number, company name, truck or delivery information, and information necessary to coordinate pickups and deliveries.

How We Use Mobile Information

Mobile telephone numbers and SMS opt-in consent information are used only for operational communications related to SRM Logistics services.

Mobile information, telephone numbers, and SMS opt-in consent data will not be sold, rented, shared, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Information may be disclosed only when required by law, to protect legal rights or safety, or to service providers acting on our behalf who are contractually required to protect the information and may not use it for marketing.

SMS Choices

Participation in the SRM Logistics SMS program is optional. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing goods, receiving services, or doing business with Specialty Rolled Metals, LLC.

Reply STOP to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.
