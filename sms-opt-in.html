SRM Logistics SMS Opt-In Information

Truck drivers and authorized logistics contacts may voluntarily provide their mobile telephone number to an SRM Logistics employee by telephone, email, or in person.

Before SMS messages are sent, the individual is informed:

By providing your mobile number, you voluntarily consent to receive recurring operational SMS messages from SRM Logistics regarding pickups, deliveries, dock or bay instructions, scheduling changes, shipment status, and missed-call follow-up. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe or HELP for assistance. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing goods, receiving services, employment, or doing business with SRM Logistics. Mobile numbers and SMS consent information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
