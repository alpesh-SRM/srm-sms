SRM Logistics SMS Terms and Conditions

Last updated: July 10, 2026

By opting in to receive SMS messages from Specialty Rolled Metals, LLC, operating as SRM Logistics, you agree to these SMS Terms and Conditions.

Program Description

SRM Logistics sends non-marketing, one-to-one operational SMS messages to truck drivers and authorized logistics contacts. Messages may concern pickup instructions, delivery instructions, dock or bay assignments, scheduling changes, shipment status, and missed-call follow-up.

Optional Consent

Participation is voluntary. Consent to receive SMS messages is not a condition of purchasing goods, receiving services, employment, or doing business with Specialty Rolled Metals, LLC.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies based on pickup, delivery, scheduling, and shipment activity.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier's plan.

Opting Out

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP. After replying STOP, you will receive a confirmation message and no further SMS messages will be sent unless you subsequently opt in again by replying START.
